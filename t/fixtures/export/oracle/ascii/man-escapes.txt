Plain Text
==========

  \\foo at the start of a paragraph.  .leading-dot remains plain.
  'leading-apostrophe remains plain.  Plain escapes: \foo, \\foo, \$,
  \{.  This paragraph ends with a backslash \ Code: `. ' \foo \\foo \$
  \{' and verbatim `. ' \foo \\foo \$ \{'.

  ,----
  | .example-dot
  | 'example-apostrophe
  | \foo \\foo \$ \{
  `----

  ,----
  | .source-dot
  | 'source-apostrophe
  | \foo \\foo \$ \{
  `----
