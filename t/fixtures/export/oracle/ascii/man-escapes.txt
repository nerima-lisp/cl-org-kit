Plain Text
==========

  .leading-dot remains plain.  'leading-apostrophe remains plain.  Plain
  escapes: \foo, \\foo, \$, \{.  Code: `. ' \foo \\foo \$ \{' and
  verbatim `. ' \foo \\foo \$ \{'.

  ,----
  | .example-dot
  | 'example-apostrophe
  | \foo \\foo \$ \{
  `----

  ,----
  | .source-dot
  | 'source-apostrophe
  | \foo \\foo \$ \{
  `----
